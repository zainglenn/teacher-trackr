## School Context

**Dubai Schools Al Khawaneej (DSK)** is a public-private partnership between the Dubai Government and Taaleem Education, opened in 2021. The school follows the American Curriculum anchored in NYSED standards, using the EngageNY ELA sequence as its planning framework (adapted for cultural appropriateness). Over 95% of the ~900-student body holds UAE nationality. Principal: Mr John Garman.

Grade 6 sits in the Middle School phase. English is taught in three parallel classes (A, B, C) with a shared year plan reviewed and approved by the HOD.
